Tune and Export popovers: five directions

Every prototype shows all five Tune tabs and all six Export tabs in dark mode, plus Tune 3D and Export Video in light mode. Real labels, ranges and default values from params.ts and settings.ts. Each panel reports its height against the 610 px between the cluster and the bottom dock at 1280×800. Sliders, tiles, knobs and pads can be dragged; the previews redraw as you drag.

Prototype only. Built against the playground at commit 6bf211d.
